Fire Emblem companion packs, built on your device
GBA Duo Companion Builder turns an exact supported ROM into a companion ZIP for GBA Duo Plus and GBA Duo Plus SS. Depending on the profile, companions include Units, Map, Talk, Growth and conditional Battle Analysis.
There are 15 supported profiles, including FE6, FE7, FE8, Project Ember, Sacred Echoes, Vision Quest and The Last Promise. Other ROM revisions need their own profiles.
How do I use a companion?
Choose your ROM, build locally, then import the downloaded ZIP through your game's Companion pack menu. Generated game images and text stay private. You can also create supported packs directly inside Plus or Plus SS.
Which emulator should I download?
GBA Duo Plus uses a second Android display. GBA Duo Plus SS puts the game and companion on one screen for phones, tablets and foldables.
